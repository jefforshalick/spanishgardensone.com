Frequently asked questions

How do I pay my monthly assessment?
  • Online: through the resident portal by e-check ($2.99 fee) or credit card (3.5% fee). On your first visit, click Register. GUD will email you a link to set your password once your account is approved.
  • By phone: call Western Alliance Bank's payment support team at (844) 739-2331.
  • By mail or bank bill pay: make checks payable to Spanish Gardens I Association, put your account number in the memo line, and mail them to:
    Spanish Gardens I Association
    c/o GUD Community Management
    P.O. Box 94915
    Las Vegas, NV 89193-4915

Don't mail payments to GUD's Mesa office; they'll be delayed. If you use your bank's online bill pay, update the payee address to the P.O. box above.

What's my account number?

It's SGD followed by your lot number, for example SGD123. If you're not sure, email info@gudhoa.com.

Can I set up automatic payments?

Yes, either through the resident portal (e-check $1.99, card 3.5%) or through Western Alliance Bank (free e-check, $5 debit card, 3% credit card). For Western Alliance, go to gudhoa.com, choose Homeowners, then "Western Alliance Bank—One-time or Recurring." You'll still receive billing statements with automatic payments.

How do I submit an architectural request?

Submit it online through the resident portal under My Property, or request a paper form from GUD Community Management at info@gudhoa.com. Before you start, review the architectural standards.
